Fly-brain drone

A small quadcopter designed to be steered by a simulated fruit fly brain.

2026
Where
A hobby project with two collaborators

Overview

Scientists have mapped the full wiring diagram of a fruit fly's brain, and a 2024 model (Shiu et al., Nature) turns that map into a simulation of about 139,000 neurons. The idea here is to let that simulated brain fly a drone.

The drone streams camera video over WiFi to a laptop running the brain model. The model's steering and escape neurons are read out and sent back as flight commands. The first target demo: wave a hand at the drone, the fly's escape circuit fires, and the drone dodges.

What I did

  • Planned the system and chose the parts: an ESP32-S3 camera board, an MPU6050 motion sensor, four 8520 brushed motors and a single-cell battery, for about $43.
  • Designed a custom X-shaped two-layer PCB, 83 mm across and about 8.6 g, that doubles as the drone's frame. A Python script generates the board files and checks its own spacing and connectivity.
  • Made an interactive 3D build guide with 23 steps, a wiring diagram and a shared plan for the team.

Results and status

  • Done: the design, the parts list and the PCB. The parts and boards are ordered.
  • Not done yet: running the brain model, assembling the drone, and flying it. Those are the next five phases of the plan.
