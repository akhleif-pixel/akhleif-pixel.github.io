Regeneron ISEF 2023

Living Together

An AI and extended-reality speech-rehabilitation system for children with congenital hearing loss. Third Award at ISEF.

When
Nov 2021 – May 2023
Where
Bethlehem, Palestine · presented in Dallas, TX
My role
Team of two, with Anas Abu A'amar
  • Unity
  • VR / AR
  • Speech recognition
  • Android

Overview

"Our Life is with You" is an integrated care system for people with chronic conditions, focused on children with congenital hearing loss. It has two parts: a web platform that connects patients, caregivers and specialists, and a mobile speech-rehabilitation app.

In the app, learners practise Arabic and English sounds, then use the words in VR games: a penalty-shootout word game and a spoken conversation in a restaurant. An AR view shows a 3D head so they can see how the mouth makes each sound.

What I did

  • Built the gamified XR rehabilitation environments in Unity with voice recognition.
  • Interviewed 153 patients and care providers and used their feedback to evaluate and improve the system.
  • Presented the research to an international panel of judges.

Results and status

  • Third Award in Behavioral and Social Sciences at the Regeneron International Science and Engineering Fair 2023 in Dallas, with a $1,000 prize.
  • In user questionnaires, 97% found the web platform beneficial, 90% found it easy to use, and 88% said it improved the work of professionals.
  • Now: rebuilding the app in Unity 6 with pronunciation scoring that runs on the phone without internet and gives feedback sound by sound, across 28 Arabic and 16 English sounds. It has not been tested with real learners yet.

What is ISEF?

The Regeneron International Science and Engineering Fair is the world's largest science and engineering competition for high school students. It is run by Society for Science, and nobody applies to it directly: students earn a place by winning a science fair affiliated with it in their own country or region.

In May 2023 the fair was held in Dallas, Texas. More than 1,600 finalists from 64 countries, regions and territories presented projects in 22 categories to panels of judges, competing for nearly $9 million in awards and scholarships.

Anas and I qualified from Palestine and presented Living Together at booth BEHA057T. Our project won a Third Award in Behavioral and Social Sciences.
