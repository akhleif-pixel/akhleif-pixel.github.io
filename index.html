About me

I was born and raised in Palestine, finished the International Baccalaureate at United World College Dilijan in Armenia, and now study Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Rochester as a Joseph C. Wilson Leader of Change Scholar.

Most of what I build sits between engineering and care, from a speech-rehabilitation system for children with hearing loss to VR exams used at a hospital bedside. I also make art from whatever is at hand: river rocks, nails and thread, scratched plexiglass.
